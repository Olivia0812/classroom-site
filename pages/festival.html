中秋節 Mid-Autumn Festival

  • mooncake（月餅）— We eat mooncakes at the Mid-Autumn Festival.
  • lantern（燈籠）— We carry a lantern under the moon.
  • reunion（團圓）— Mid-Autumn Festival is a time for family reunion.
